Changes made to OpenNGC: only the Messier objects and the NGC and IC objects
of magnitude 12 or brighter are kept (from the NGC.csv and addendum.csv files,
without the duplicate record of M102, the non-existent objects, the stars, the
novae, the dark nebulae and the components of galaxies such as NED01), the
columns are renamed and reduced, the right ascensions are in decimal hours, the
type codes are replaced by Skymap types, the magnitude is the V magnitude, or
the B magnitude when there is none, and its band is added.
