data/stars.bin

This file merges the Yale Bright Star Catalogue (Hoffleit & Warren, 1991) with
data of Tycho-2 (Høg et al., 2000), of the new reduction of Hipparcos (van
Leeuwen, 2007) and of Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration, 2023), adapted and packed
into a binary format by bin/build_stars.
